# Opening the App

There are no accounts to create on this site.

## Open the site

Open the site address in a browser. The home screen shows a verse of the day and two buttons, *Read Bible* and *Search*. Next, see [Your First Study Session](./quick-start.md).

## Your settings

Your settings, tabs, and reading position are stored in this browser on this device. They don't carry over to other browsers or devices. Clearing the browser's site data clears them.
