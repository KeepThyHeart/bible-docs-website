# Your First Study Session

A short tour: open a passage, read commentary on it, and try both kinds of search.

## 1. Start at the home screen

The home screen shows a verse of the day. Click it to go to that verse in context. Below it are two buttons, *Read Bible* and *Search*.

![The home screen with the verse of the day and the Read Bible / Search buttons](/img/web/quick-start-home-votd.png)

Click *Read Bible*.

## 2. Pick a book and chapter

To open the book and chapter picker, click the current reference at the top of the reading area (for example, "Psalm 23"), or click the *Table of Contents* button (the list icon) in the header.

![The book and chapter picker open over the reading area](/img/web/quick-start-book-chapter-picker.png)

Choose a book, then a chapter. You can also type a reference such as `John 3`, `Psalm 23`, or `Gen 1:1-5` in the search bar. See [Reading the Bible](../user-guide/bible-reading.md).

## 3. Read a chapter

*← Prev* and *Next →* at the bottom of the chapter go to the previous and next chapter, across book boundaries. The same chevrons are at the right end of the toolbar. To change the text size or theme, see [Personalizing Your Experience](../user-guide/personalizing.md).

## 4. Click a verse to study it

Click a verse to select it. The panels on the right update to show commentary, cross-references, topics, and original-language information for that verse. On a phone, the Study and Commentary screens update instead.

![A chapter of John with a verse selected and the commentary panel populated](/img/web/quick-start-chapter-with-commentary.png)

See [Study Tools](../user-guide/study-tools.md).

## 5. Try both kinds of search

The search bar is at the top of the screen. Press <kbd>/</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac) to focus it. The button to its left shows the search mode. Click it to choose *Standard Search* (magnifying glass) or *Ideas Search* (lightbulb).

![The search bar with a query typed in and the mode button visible](/img/web/quick-start-search-bar-query.png)

With *Standard Search*, type `the good shepherd`. The results list every verse containing those words. Click a result to go to it.

With *Ideas Search*, type `a shepherd boy killed a giant with a sling`. Ideas Search attempts to match by meaning rather than by exact words.

See [Searching](../user-guide/search.md) for more, including Strong's number searches. The "?" button in the header opens a short in-app help panel.
