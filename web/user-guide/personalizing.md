# Personalizing Your Experience

## Opening settings

The gear icon in the header takes you to Settings. The settings panel has these tabs: *Text Size*, *Theme*, *Modules*, *Gestures*, *Offline*, and *About*. Close it with <kbd>Escape</kbd> or by clicking outside it. Changes save immediately; there is no Save button.

Settings are stored in this browser on this device. They don't carry over to other browsers or devices.

![The settings panel open on the Theme tab, showing the colour theme grid](/img/web/personalizing-settings-theme.png)

## Themes

The *Theme* tab shows fifteen color themes as swatches: *Light*, *Dark*, and *Sepia*, followed by Arctic, Autumn, Forest, Lagoon, Meadow, Midnight, Ocean, Parchment, Rose, Slate, Sunrise, and Sunset. A theme applies immediately.

*Auto* follows your device's light or dark setting.

The theme button in the header offers Light, Dark, and Sepia directly, and *More…* opens the full grid.

![The same passage shown in the Light theme and the Dark theme](/img/web/personalizing-theme-comparison.png)

## Fonts

The *Theme* tab also has font schemes. Each scheme pairs a heading font with a body font, previewed on its card. A scheme applies to the Bible text, headings, and study panels.

- **Recommended:** Classic (Georgia), Lora, Merriweather, Crimson Pro, and Libre Baskerville.
- **Other:** Times, Modern (Sans-Serif), EB Garamond, Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond, Spectral, and Book Antiqua.

![The font scheme cards on the Theme tab, grouped into Recommended and Other](/img/web/personalizing-font-selector.png)

## Text size

The *Text Size* tab has a plus/minus control that scales the Bible text, study panels, and interface together. *Advanced* shows separate sliders:

- *Bible Text* — the Bible text.
- *Study Text* — commentaries, cross-references, and the other study panels.
- *UI* — menus, buttons, and labels.
- *Bible Line Height* and *Study Line Height* — the spacing between lines.

![The Text Size tab with the plus/minus control and the advanced sliders](/img/web/personalizing-text-size.png)

The tab also has:

- *Words of Christ in Red* — affects the reading area only, in translations that include red-letter data.
- *Interlinear Layout* — in Study mode, shows original-language words either inline in parentheses or stacked in columns beneath each English word.

## Modules

The *Modules* tab controls which study content is shown.

- *Show combined commentary overview tab* turns the [Commentary Combined Summary](./study-tools.md#commentary-combined-summary) on or off.
- Below it, you can choose which topical indexes are active.

The available translations and commentaries are chosen by the site operator.

## Gestures

The *Gestures* tab controls touch behavior.

- *Swipe to change chapters* — a horizontal swipe across the Bible pane goes to the previous or next chapter.
- Two thresholds set how far a swipe must travel: one for the Bible pane, and one for swiping between verses in the Commentary pane. Raise them if swipes trigger by accident; lower them if swipes don't register.

See [Using on Your Phone](./mobile.md).

## Left-handed mode

Left-handed mode, on the *Theme* tab, flips the bottom navigation bar. It affects the mobile layout only.

## About

The *About* tab shows the version and license ({{licenseName}}), a link to the source code, and a *Refresh App* button.
