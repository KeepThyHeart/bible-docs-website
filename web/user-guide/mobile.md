# Using on Your Phone

On a phone, each part of the app has its own screen instead of side-by-side panels. A bar at the bottom switches between screens.

## The mobile layout

The Bible text fills most of the screen. The bottom navigation bar has five tabs.

![The Bible text on a phone with the bottom navigation bar visible](/img/web/mobile-bible-with-nav.png)

## The bottom navigation bar

From left to right:

- **Home** — the welcome screen with the verse of the day.
- **Study** — cross-references, topics, and interlinear words for the current verse.
- **Read** — the Bible text.
- **Cmt** — commentary for the current verse.
- **Search** — the search results screen.

Tap a tab to switch screens. Left-handed mode flips the bar, and it is available on the *Theme* tab in Settings; see [Personalizing Your Experience](./personalizing.md).

![The bottom navigation bar with its five tabs](/img/web/mobile-bottom-nav.png)

## The Study screen

The Study tab shows cross-references, topics, and the interlinear for the verse you last selected, as one scrolling page. All three sections are expanded. *Browse All Topics*, at the end of the topics section, opens the full topic browser.

The header at the top names the verse. Tap it to expand it. The expanded header shows:

- The full verse text.
- A thumbtack that keeps the screen on this verse while you read elsewhere.
- Previous and next verse buttons.
- A dropdown of verses you have viewed.

![The mobile Study screen showing the cross-references and topics sections](/img/web/mobile-study-screen.png)

## The Commentary screen

The "Cmt" tab lists the commentaries that have an entry for the current verse, as cards. Each card shows the commentary's name, a word count, and a preview. Tap a card to read the full entry. Swipe or tap back to return to the list.

- The *Find commentary…* box filters by name.
- Use *Starred*, *All*, and *Muted* to filter the list.
- The star on a card keeps that commentary at the top of the list. Muting a commentary hides it. (It can be un-muted at the bottom of the list.)

![The mobile Commentary screen showing the card list](/img/web/mobile-commentary-screen.png)

## Touch gestures

**Swipe between chapters.** Swipe horizontally across the Bible text to go to the previous or next chapter. The *Gestures* tab in Settings sets how far a swipe must travel, and can turn the gesture off.

**Swipe between verses.** On the Commentary screen, the same gesture moves between verses. It has its own threshold setting.

## Auto-hiding header

The header hides when you scroll down and reappears when you scroll up.
