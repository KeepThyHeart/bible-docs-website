# Reading the Bible

This page covers the reading area, navigation, translations, tabs, and display modes.

## The reading area

The Bible text fills the main part of the window, with verse numbers as small markers beside it. The toolbar above the text holds the controls for the current passage. *← Prev* and *Next →* buttons below the last verse move between chapters.

![The reading area in Standard mode showing Psalm 23 in the KJV](/img/web/bible-reading-standard-mode.png)

## Navigating to a passage

**Book and chapter picker.** Click the current reference at the top of the reading area (for example, "Psalm 23"), or the *Table of Contents* button (the list icon) in the header. Choose a book, then a chapter.

![The book and chapter picker open, showing the book grid](/img/web/bible-reading-book-chapter-picker.png)

**Search bar.** Type a reference such as `John 3:16`, `Romans 8`, `Gen 1:1-5`, or `Ps 23`, and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. References are recognized automatically, including near-misses like `jonh 3:16`. See [Searching](./search.md).

**Chapter navigation.** *← Prev* and *Next →* below the last verse, and the chevrons at the right end of the toolbar, move one chapter at a time. They cross book boundaries; for example, *Next →* at the end of Malachi goes to Matthew.

![The Prev / Next chapter buttons below the end of a chapter](/img/web/bible-reading-chapter-nav.png)

**History.** The toolbar's back and forward arrows move through your history, as in a browser. The history button beside them lists recent passages.

After you follow a cross-reference or a link in a commentary, a "Back to …" bar appears above the text. Click it to return. It disappears after about 30 seconds, or you can dismiss it with ✕.

## Switching translations

The toolbar button showing the translation abbreviation (KJV, ASV, and so on) opens the *Select Translation* dialog. It lists the site's translations, with a filter box at the top. Switching translations keeps you on the same passage.

![The Select Translation dialog open with the filter box and grouped list](/img/web/bible-reading-translation-dropdown.png)

## Multiple tabs

Tabs along the top of the reading area hold separate passages.

- The **+** button at the end of the tab bar opens a new tab.
- Click a tab to switch to it. Drag tabs left or right to reorder them.
- The ✕ on a tab closes it.
- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+click (<kbd>Cmd</kbd>+click on a Mac) a search result or a Bible reference in a commentary to open it in a new tab.

Tabs are remembered for a while in that same browser for when you come back to the site.

![Several Bible tabs open along the top of the reading area](/img/web/bible-reading-multiple-tabs.png)

## Display modes

The dropdown in the toolbar switches between three display modes.

*Standard* is the default. Each verse is marked with its number.

![The same passage in Standard mode, each verse marked with its number](/img/web/bible-reading-mode-standard.png)

*Reading* shows the text as paragraphs, without verse numbers.

![The same passage in Reading mode: flowing paragraphs, no verse numbers](/img/web/bible-reading-mode-reading.png)

*Study* adds the original Greek or Hebrew beneath the reading language, with Strong's numbers you can hover over or click.  However, this requires a Bible translation that has interlinear data provided (which many don't).  It adds two checkboxes:

- *Interlinear* shows the original-language words.
- *Notes* shows the translation publisher's notes, if the translation includes them.

Many translations have no interlinear data or notes. The app shows a message when they're missing. See [Study Tools](./study-tools.md).

![The same passage in Study mode with interlinear words and Strong's numbers visible](/img/web/bible-reading-mode-study.png)

## Words of Christ in red

Open Settings (the gear icon in the header), go to the *Text Size* tab, and turn on *Words of Christ in Red*. This affects the reading area only, and only in translations that include red-letter data. See [Personalizing Your Experience](./personalizing.md).
