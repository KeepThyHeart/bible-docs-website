# Searching

The search bar finds words, finds passages by meaning, goes to references, and looks up Strong's numbers.

## The search bar

The search bar is at the top of every screen. Click it, or press <kbd>/</kbd>, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>G</kbd> (<kbd>Cmd</kbd> on a Mac). The keyboard shortcuts also select any text already in the box.

The button to the left of the box shows the current mode: a magnifying glass labeled *Search*, or a lightbulb labeled *Ideas*. Click it to choose *Standard Search* (exact words) or *Ideas Search* (meaning). The chosen mode is remembered.

![The search mode menu open, showing Standard Search and Ideas Search](/img/web/search-mode-menu.png)

## Standard Search

*Standard Search* finds verses containing the words you type, in the translation you are reading. For example, `shepherd` returns every verse that contains that word. Results open in a panel beside the text. Each result shows the reference, the translation, and a snippet with the search words highlighted.

If nothing matches, the panel offers to run the search in another translation or as an Ideas search.

![Standard Search results for a query, in the results panel beside the Bible text](/img/web/search-keyword-results.png)

## Ideas Search

*Ideas Search* finds passages by meaning. Describe what you are looking for in plain English. For example, `a shepherd boy killed a giant with a sling` finds David and Goliath in 1 Samuel 17, even though typing that as a keyword search would find no results.  This can also help you find half-remembered verses, or passages you vaguely remember but for which you don't recall the specific wording.  It doesn't always give accurate results, but often, it can help you find what you are looking for.

When an Ideas search also has exact-word matches, a banner offers to show them.

![Ideas Search results for a paraphrased query](/img/web/search-semantic-results.png)

## Jumping to a reference

Type a reference (`John 3:16`, `Romans 8`, `Gen 1:1-5`, `Ps 23`) in either mode to go to it instead of searching. See [Reading the Bible](./bible-reading.md) for other ways to navigate.

## Strong's number search

Type a Strong's number to find every verse that uses that Greek or Hebrew word. Greek numbers look like `G25` and Hebrew like `H7225`. The form `strongs:G25` also works.

The results show:

- A header with the number, original word, transliteration, and a short gloss (explanation of the word's meaning from the translator).
- The verses that contain the word.
- Word family pills: related words built on the same root. Click one to search that word instead.
- An *Include related words* toggle, which searches the whole family at once. For example, the verb *agapao*, the noun *agape*, and the adjective *agapetos*.

In Study mode, you can also click a Strong's number under an interlinear word and choose *Search all occurrences*. See [Study Tools](./study-tools.md).

![Strong's search results with the word header and word family pills](/img/web/search-strongs-results.png)

## Working with results

- Click a result to open it in the current tab. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+click (<kbd>Cmd</kbd>+click) opens it in a new Bible tab.
- Long result lists load in batches. *Show more results* loads the next batch; *Load all* loads the rest.
- The ✕ closes the results panel and returns to the study and commentary panels.
- On a phone, results have their own screen in the bottom navigation bar. See [Using on Your Phone](./mobile.md).

Search requires the server and is unavailable offline. The panel shows a message when you are offline.
