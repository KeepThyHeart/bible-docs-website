# Quick Start

This is a tour of the main window. If you haven't installed the app yet, see [Installation](./installation.md).

## The main window

On first launch, the app opens to John 3 by default. The window has three parts:

- **Bible pane** (left): the Bible text. Its header holds the translation selector, the book and chapter picker, and the display controls.
- **Study pane** (right): tabs for commentaries, dictionaries, books, notes, documents, the journal, prayer lists, search results, and topics. It can be collapsed to show the Bible text at full width.
- **Search bar** (top): searches, navigates to references, and runs commands.

![The main window on first launch: the Bible pane, the study pane, and the search bar](/img/desktop/quick-start-main-window.png)

## 1. Go to a passage

Click the reference in the Bible pane header ("John 3") to open the book and chapter picker. Choose a book, then a chapter.

![The book and chapter picker open over the reading area, with the book list and the chapter buttons](/img/desktop/quick-start-book-chapter-picker.png)

Other ways to navigate:

- Type a reference such as `Romans 8:28` in the search bar and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.
- <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Down</kbd> and <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Up</kbd> go to the next and previous chapter.
- <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Left</kbd> and <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Right</kbd> go back and forward through your history, as in a web browser.

## 2. Choose a translation

The dropdown at the top left of the Bible pane lists your installed translations. Changing it keeps you on the same passage. Translations in a language other than the interface language have a language prefix, such as `[ESP]` for Spanish if your interface is in another language like English.

![The translation selector open, listing the installed translations](/img/desktop/quick-start-translation-dropdown.png)

## 3. Try the display modes

The Bible pane header has three display modes:

| Mode | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Simple** | Verse numbers and text only. |
| **Standard** | Each verse gets its own line, and any footnote markers, cross-reference indicators, and section headings are also displayed (depends on what the Bible module has). |
| **Study** | Adds interlinear Greek or Hebrew, Strong's numbers, and morphology codes, as well as links to commentaries and cross-references. |

Interlinear data is shown only for modules which contain this information. See [Study Tools](../user-guide/study-tools.md).

![The same passage in all three display modes, side by side](/img/desktop/quick-start-display-modes.png)

## 4. Select a verse

Click a verse to select it. The study pane follows the selection: the Commentary tab shows entries for that verse, and the Topics tab shows the topics it belongs to.

![A verse selected in the Bible pane, with the Commentary tab showing entries for it](/img/desktop/quick-start-verse-with-commentary.png)

## 5. Mark up the text

Right-click a verse to open the verse context menu. From there, you can copy the passage (this opens the copy dialog), add a note, apply a color or underline, or clear markup that you applied previously.

![The verse context menu open, showing its actions](/img/desktop/quick-start-verse-context-menu.png)

## 6. Write a note

With a verse selected, open the *Verse Notes* tab in the study pane and start a new note. Notes save automatically as you type; there is no save button. Verses with notes show a note icon in the Bible pane.

![A note holding an attributed quotation about the verse, with the note icon showing in the Bible pane](/img/desktop/quick-start-verse-note.png)

## 7. Search

Click the search bar or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>L</kbd>, and type a word or phrase. Suggestions appear after three characters. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to open the full results in the study pane. The results include a graph of which books the matches occur in. Click a result to go to that verse.

![Search results opened below the Bible text](/img/desktop/quick-start-search-results.png)

## 8. Rearrange the window

Right-click a tab in the study pane to split it right or down, pop it out into its own window, or close it. You can also drag tabs between groups. The *Layout* dropdown restores one of three built-in layouts: Study Mode, Reading Mode, or Study Mode (Quad). See [Sessions & Layout](../user-guide/sessions-and-layout.md).

![The Layout dropdown open, showing the built-in presets](/img/desktop/quick-start-layout-dropdown.png)

## 9. Close the app

The current passage, open tabs, layout, and notes are saved automatically as a session. When you reopen the app, it restores them.
