# Personalizing

Open *File → Preferences…* (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>,</kbd>), or *Bible → Preferences…* on macOS. The dialog has sections for General, Typography, Fonts, Themes, Extensions, and Diagnostics.

![The Preferences dialog open on its Themes section](/img/desktop/personalizing-preferences-themes.png)

## Themes

There are three themes: Light (the default), Dark, and Sepia. Switch themes from *View → Theme* or from the Themes section of Preferences.

## Text settings per pane

The Bible, commentary, dictionary, and book panes each have their own text settings, opened from the pane header:

- Font family
- Font size
- Line height, from compact to spacious
- Red-letter text (the words of Jesus Christ in red)

A live sample updates as you change them.

![The Typography section of Preferences, with its live sample](/img/desktop/personalizing-text-settings.png)

## Global scaling

The General section has two scaling settings:

- A global font scale (from 70% to 150%) that scales text in all content panes together.
- An interface text size for buttons, labels, and menus, separate from content.

Window zoom (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>+</kbd>, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>-</kbd>, and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>0</kbd> to reset) scales the whole window, including the interface. The global font scale affects only content text.

## Typography and fonts

The Typography and Fonts sections set text sizes, line heights, and font families for all panes at once.

## Keyboard shortcuts

See [Keyboard Shortcuts](./keyboard-shortcuts.md), or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>/</kbd> in the app.

## Extensions

The Extensions section lists installed extensions.
