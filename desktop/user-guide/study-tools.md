# Study Tools

The study tools follow the verse selected in the Bible pane. Commentaries, dictionaries, cross-references, and topics update when the selection changes.

![A verse selected in the Bible pane, with the study pane showing content for it](/img/desktop/study-tools-verse-selected.png)

## Commentaries

The Commentary tab shows the entries for your installed commentaries for the selected verse.

- You can open multiple commentaries at once and switch between them.
- The previous and next arrows jump to the nearest verse that has an entry in that commentary.
- Scripture references in commentary text are links that link to the Bible pane.

![The Commentary tab with an entry open and the commentary tab bar above it](/img/desktop/study-tools-commentary-entry.png)

Install and remove commentaries through *File → Module Manager*. See [Installing Modules](../getting-started/installing-modules.md).

## Dictionaries and lexicons

In the Dictionary tab, choose a dictionary from the dropdown, such as a Strong's lexicon or a Bible dictionary. Browse entries alphabetically or search for a term, and click an entry to see it. Depending on the dictionary, an entry will includes the word, transliteration, pronunciation, definition, and usage examples.

![The Dictionary tab showing a Strong’s lexicon entry](/img/desktop/study-tools-dictionary-strongs.png)

### Strong's numbers

Strong's numbers refer to Hebrew and Greek words of the Bible. Greek numbers start with *G* (e.g., G26 is *agape*, love). Hebrew numbers start with *H* (e.g., H430 is *Elohim*, God). In Study display mode, the numbers appear under the original-language words. You can click one to look it up.

### Word occurrences

From a dictionary entry, you can list every verse where that word occurs (if the active Bible module contains this information).

![A Strong’s search listing every verse where the word occurs](/img/desktop/study-tools-dictionary-occurrences.png)

## Study mode and the interlinear

Select Study from the display mode control in the Bible pane header. This shows interlinear data (if available for that Bible module).

![The interlinear stack for a single verse: original word, transliteration, Strong’s number, morphology](/img/desktop/study-tools-interlinear-stack.png)

Study mode requires interlinear data, which many Bible modules to not have.  To use it, install a Bible module that includes interlinear data.

## Cross-references

In Standard and Study modes, cross-reference markers appear beside verses. Click a marker to see related passages, and click a passage to go to it.

You can also create your own cross-references between verses. They are included in backups.

![The Cross-References section expanded, listing related passages](/img/desktop/study-tools-cross-references.png)

## Topics

The Topics tab browses topical indexes: subject headings with their verses, arranged in a hierarchy.

- The search box at the top finds a topic by name.
- Broad topics have sub-topics.
- The verses for a topic are listed beneath it. Click one to go to it.
- When you select a verse in the Bible pane, the Topics tab lists the topics that include it.

![The Topics tab showing a topic with its sub-topics and verses](/img/desktop/study-tools-topics.png)

## The Study pane

The Study tab collects the commentaries, cross-references, and topics for the selected verse in one place.

![The Study pane gathering commentary, cross-references, and topics for one verse](/img/desktop/study-tools-study-pane.png)

## Books and devotionals

The Books tab shows books such as devotionals, theological books, and study guides, with a tree view of the table of contents. References to Bible verses are linked so you can click them and view the text of the verses.

To search these modules, see [Searching](./search.md). To record what you find, see [Notes & Highlights](./notes-and-highlights.md).
