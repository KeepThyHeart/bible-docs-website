# Bookmarks

Bookmarks are kept in a list and can be renamed and reordered.  For example, you might have one bookmark for personal devotions and another for a church Bible study.

## Adding a bookmark

Select a verse, open the bookmark ribbon in the Bible pane's toolbar, and choose *Add Current Verse*. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>D</kbd> adds the bookmark directly, without opening the list.

If the selected verse is already bookmarked, the first item in the list reads *Remove Bookmark* instead.

To bookmark a passage, select the range first: click the first verse, then shift-click the last.

You can right-click a verse or selected passage and choose the option to add it to your bookmarks. This menu can also move an existing bookmark; see [Moving a bookmark](#moving-a-bookmark).

<!-- shot: bookmarks-toolbar-star — The Bible toolbar with the bookmark ribbon filled, and a bookmarked verse in the text -->

## Going to a bookmark

Open the bookmark ribbon in the toolbar and click a bookmark. The Bible pane goes to it.

Bookmarked verses show a small red ribbon beside them in the text.

<!-- shot: bookmarks-jump-list — The bookmark jump list open below the toolbar -->

## Naming a bookmark

An unnamed bookmark shows its reference, such as *John 3:16* or *Romans 8:28-39*. To name one, open *Manage Bookmarks* and click the edit icon (pencil) beside it.

## Moving a bookmark

Right-click a verse and hover the command that adds it to your bookmarks. A submenu lists your existing bookmarks. Choose one to point it at this verse instead of creating a new bookmark.

- A **named** bookmark keeps its name. For example, a bookmark named *Where I'm reading in Romans* keeps that name and now points to Romans 9:1 instead of Romans 5:12.
- An **unnamed** bookmark changes its label to the new reference.

<!-- shot: bookmarks-replace-menu — The verse context menu showing existing bookmarks as replacement targets -->

## Managing bookmarks

*Manage Bookmarks*, at the bottom of the bookmark list or in the command palette, opens the full list. From there you can:

- **Rename** a bookmark, or clear its name to show the reference again.
- **Reorder** bookmarks by dragging the handle on the left. With the keyboard, focus the handle and press the up and down arrow keys. The list is never re-sorted automatically.
- **Remove** a bookmark.
- **Go to** a bookmark by clicking its name. This closes the dialog.

<kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels the edits and closes the dialog.

<!-- shot: bookmarks-manage-dialog — The Manage Bookmarks dialog with several named and unnamed bookmarks -->

## Backups

Bookmarks are stored with your notes and highlights and are included in backups. See [Backup & Restore](./backup-and-restore.md).
