# Reading the Bible

This page covers how to read the Bible with this app, including navigation, translations, display modes, and reading several passages at once.

## The Bible pane

By default, verse numbers appear as numbers beside the text. The pane header holds the translation selector, the current reference, the navigation arrows, and the display mode controls.

![The Bible pane in Standard mode, showing Psalm 23](/img/desktop/bible-reading-standard-mode.png)

## Navigating

**Book and chapter picker.** Click the current reference in the header. Choose a book from the list, then a chapter number.

![The book and chapter picker open, showing the book list and the grid of chapter buttons](/img/desktop/bible-reading-book-chapter-picker.png)

**Search bar.** Type a reference (`John 3:16`, `Romans 8`, `Gen 1:1-5`) and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. See [Searching](./search.md).

**Chapter arrows.** The left and right arrows in the header move one chapter at a time, across book boundaries. <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Down</kbd> and <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Up</kbd> go to the next and previous chapter. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>G</kbd> opens a go-to-verse prompt.

**History.** <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Left</kbd> goes back and <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Right</kbd> goes forward, as in a web browser. For example, after following a cross-reference, going back returns you to the previous passage.

## Selecting a verse

Click a verse number to select it. The study pane (commentary, dictionary, cross-references, topics, and notes) updates to the selected verse. To select a range, click across several verses.

## Switching translations

The dropdown at the top left of the pane lists your installed translations. Switching keeps you on the same passage. Translations in a language other than the interface language have a language prefix. The filter box at the top of the list finds a translation by name.

![The translation selector open, with its filter box and the installed translations](/img/desktop/bible-reading-translation-dropdown.png)

## Parallel view

The *Parallel View* button in the pane header shows translations side by side, one column per translation, aligned by verse.  You can have up to 4 translations displayed at once.

![Parallel view with two translations in columns against the same verses](/img/desktop/bible-reading-parallel-view.png)

## Display modes

The pane header has three display modes.

**Simple** shows verse numbers and text only.

![A passage in Simple mode: verse numbers and text only](/img/desktop/bible-reading-mode-simple.png)

**Standard** gives each verse its own line.  If the translation contains footnote markers, cross-reference indicators, and section headings, those are shown also.

![The same passage in Standard mode, with its footnote and cross-reference markers](/img/desktop/bible-reading-mode-standard.png)

**Study** shows each word with its original-language form (if provided by that particular translation's module), transliteration, Strong's number, and morphology code. It requires interlinear data; see [Study Tools](./study-tools.md).

![The same passage in Study mode, showing the interlinear stack under each word](/img/desktop/bible-reading-mode-study.png)

## Multiple passages

The Bible pane supports multiple tabs. Open a new tab from the tab bar, click a tab to switch to it, drag tabs to reorder them, and close a tab with its close button. Tabs are saved with the session.

You can also split the Bible pane or pop a passage out into its own window. See [Sessions & Layout](./sessions-and-layout.md).

## Text appearance

Font, text size, line height, and red-letter text for the words of Christ are adjustable. See [Personalizing](./personalizing.md).
