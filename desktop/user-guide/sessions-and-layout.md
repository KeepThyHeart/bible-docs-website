# Sessions & Layout

Panes can be split, dragged, resized, and popped out into separate windows. The arrangement is saved automatically.

## Sessions

A session holds the current passage, open tabs, pane arrangement, and your position in each pane. It is saved continuously; there is no manual save. After the app closes, the next launch restores the session.

## The default arrangement

By default, the Bible pane is on the left and the study pane is on the right. The control on the study pane's edge collapses and expands it.

![The study pane collapsed, giving the Bible text the full window](/img/desktop/layout-study-pane-collapsed.png)

## Rearranging panes

Right-click a tab to:

- *Split Right*: move the pane to a new group to the right.
- *Split Down*: move the pane to a new group below.
- *Pop Out to Window*: move the pane to its own window.
- Close the tab.

You can also drag tabs between groups, drag a tab to the edge of a group to create a split, and drag the dividers between groups to resize them.

![A pane’s options menu, offering Split Right, Split Down, and Pop Out to Window](/img/desktop/layout-tab-context-menu.png)

## Built-in layouts

The *Layout* dropdown resets the window to one of three layouts.

| Preset | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| **Study Mode** | Bible on the left, study tools on the right. The default. |
| **Reading Mode** | Bible only, with margins; everything else collapsed. |
| **Study Mode (Quad)** | A 2×2 grid — commentary top-left, Bible top-right, dictionaries bottom-left, notes bottom-right. |

Applying a layout rearranges your existing tabs instead of closing them, and offers to undo the change.

![The Layout dropdown open, showing each preset with its description](/img/desktop/layout-dropdown-presets.png)

## Popping panes out

A popped-out pane moves to its own window. This lets you spread the app across multiple monitors if you have that kind of setup.

Popped-out windows stay linked to the main window. Selecting a verse in the Bible pane updates a popped-out commentary.

![A Commentary pane popped out into a window of its own](/img/desktop/layout-popped-out-window.png)

## Zoom

<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>+</kbd> zooms in, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>-</kbd> zooms out, and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>0</kbd> resets to actual size. Zoom scales the whole interface. To change only the Bible text size, see [Personalizing](./personalizing.md).

## Resetting the layout

Choose a layout from the *Layout* dropdown. Notes and highlights are not affected.
